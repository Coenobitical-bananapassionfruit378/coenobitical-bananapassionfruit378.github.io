🚀 What is SeshNet?

SeshNet is a lightweight, privacy-focused desktop application that shows you exactly how much internet data each program on your computer uses — in real time. Whether you want to stop a game from hogging bandwidth, block a background service you don't trust, or just see what's connecting to the internet, SeshNet puts you in control. Everything runs locally on your device, so your data stays private.

💡 How to Use SeshNet

Once SeshNet is running, you'll see a clean dashboard with each application that's using the internet. Here's what you can do:

  • View live data usage: Each app shows its current upload and download speed, plus total data used.
  • Block an app: Click the block button next to any app. You can choose to block it for a specific time (e.g., 1 hour) or permanently.
  • Unblock an app: Go to the blocked list and click unblock to restore internet access.
  • Stop background services: Look for services you don't recognize and block them to save bandwidth.

🔧 Why You Need SeshNet

Many applications and services use your internet connection without you knowing. Windows updates, telemetry, game launchers, and even some antivirus software can consume large amounts of data in the background. SeshNet gives you transparency and control:

  • Improve privacy: See which apps are sending data and stop them.
